## Mod Information
PHIGHTING! THE BLACKROCK DIVISION is a mindustry mod made by BlueDoves3821.

The mod is obviously based on the blackrock region from the roblox game: **PHIGHTING!**

The KORBLOX Administration rules over blackrock, and you're given you a task:
you're given a blackrock token factory to produce blackrock tokens.
<p align="center">
  <img src="sprites/blocks/production/blackrock-token-factory.png" width="120">
</p>
  
exchange the blackrock tokens for other tokens in order to build other structures.
such as, a blackrock turret:
<p align="center">
  <img src="sprites/blocks/turret/blackrock-turret-preview.png" width="120">
</p>

a hyperlaser wireframer & turret that synergizes with eachother.
<p align="center">
  <img src="sprites/blocks/turret/hyperlaser-wireframer.png" width="120">
  <img src="sprites/blocks/turret/hyperlaser-turret-preview.png" width="120">
</p>

or a beta biograft overdriver for large damage.
<p align="center">
  <img src="sprites/units/biograft-beta-overdriver-full.png" width="120">
</p>

Use these structures alongside your base, and soon, the sector will be yours.
